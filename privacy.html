✅ CoatPro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your information never leaves your device.

1. Overview

CoatPro ("the App") is a professional floor coating guide that provides product information, coverage calculators, and job tracking tools for flooring contractors and DIY enthusiasts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

By using CoatPro, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required
  • No name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information is collected
  • No location data is collected or tracked
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking is performed
  • No advertising identifiers are used

3. Camera Access

CoatPro requests access to your device's camera solely for AI product scanning — to identify floor coating products and retrieve product specifications. Camera images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded, stored remotely, or transmitted to any server. Camera access is only active while you are actively using the scan feature.

4. Local Data Storage

Your job records, saved calculations, and app preferences are stored locally on your device only using AsyncStorage (a standard local storage mechanism on mobile devices). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not synced to any cloud service
  • Is not accessible to Max's AI Solutions or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing App data in your device settings

5. Pro & Business Subscriptions

CoatPro offers optional Pro and Business subscription tiers with enhanced features. All subscription purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Max's AI Solutions does not collect, process, or store any payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles payment and billing data.

6. Third-Party Services

CoatPro does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers. The only third-party service used is Google Play Billing for subscription management, as described above. We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with any third party.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

CoatPro is designed to be safe for users of all ages. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, the App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, or any age group.

8. Data Security

Because CoatPro does not transmit any data to external servers, there is no risk of your data being intercepted or compromised through the App. All data remains on your device under the security controls provided by your operating system.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users through an in-app notice or update the App listing on Google Play. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
